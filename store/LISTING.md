# Cascade Keys: store listing

**Title:** Cascade Keys

**Tagline:** Tap in time. Hear the piano.

**Description:**
Tap four lanes in time with melodies by composers who died before 1925 and traditional folk songs. Every correct tap plays the next note on a synthesised piano. Hit holds and doubles, watch the tempo climb, and earn up to three stars a song. Arcade mode has no end. The daily song is the same for everyone, once a day. Your scores stay on this device.

**Modes:**
- Songs: 27 public-domain tunes, each with a star rating.
- Arcade: endless tiles, a combo and a score. No fail.
- Daily Challenge: one song per UTC day, played once. The first result goes to the leaderboard.

**Thumbnail:** `web/public/thumbnail.svg`

**Launch:** the Play button opens the mode choice (Songs, Arcade, Daily Challenge).
